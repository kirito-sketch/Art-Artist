# V30: structural iteration

Evidence: *Art & Artist V29 Usability Research Synthesis* (1 Oct 2026; 43 sessions, 1,366 events,
67 feedback records, 85 task attempts). Later-task samples were n = 3, so they are treated as warnings,
not conclusions.

V30 is a structural iteration, not "V29 plus every participant suggestion". The proposition tested
well (7 of 8 first-run respondents rated understanding 4 or 5 out of 5). The problems were orientation,
prioritisation and activation. Every change below names the research problem it answers; anything
without a good answer was not built.

## The model

**Home answers "what matters to me right now?". Discover answers "let me look for something".**

What matters depends on two things:

- **Who you are:** primary craft, other crafts, city, availability and network.
- **What you are trying to do right now (intent):**
  - Find work
  - Find people for a project
  - Collaborate
  - Grow my network
  - Just exploring

Intent is a state, not a persona: the same person changes it as their work changes. One adaptable
system (`src/domain/relevance.ts`) replaces the idea of a Home per craft. The layout is the same for
everyone. Intent changes the **order** of Home's four sections and the **ranking** inside each one,
and every block says why it is there.

| Intent | Home order |
|---|---|
| Find work | Opportunities → Active projects → People → Activity |
| Collaborate | Opportunities (collaborations first) → People → Projects → Activity |
| Find people for a project | People (other crafts, available and nearby first; Save) → Activity → Projects → Opportunities |
| Grow my network | People → Activity → Projects → Opportunities |
| Just exploring | Activity → Projects → People → Opportunities |

Within each section, ranking works as follows:

- **Opportunities:** primary craft weighs most, then other crafts, then city, then what the intent
  asks for (collaborations for *Collaborate*, paid work for *Find work*).
- **People:**
  - *Find people* and *Collaborate*: favour other crafts, available, nearby.
  - *Find work*: favours your own craft and the people who hire.
  - *Grow my network* and *Just exploring*: favour your craft and city.

Defaults:

- Directors and producers start on *Find people for a project*; everyone else on *Find work*.
- Onboarding asks.
- Home's intent bar ("Director of Photography in Hyderabad · showing what matters for: Find work ·
  Change") switches it in one tap.

## Changes, and the problem each one solves

### P0: Functional failures

| Fix | Research problem |
|---|---|
| Every craft on the onboarding list now has at least one opportunity: 8 new seed roles (editor, colourist, VFX, production, director, production design / costume / make-up, sound / music, writing) on existing houses and people. Verified for all 15 crafts | "Suggested roles missing after onboarding": V29 had roles only for cinematography, acting, lighting and camera |
| Respond sheet and Collaboration composer no longer crash for a new account with no work (they assumed a portfolio existed) | Blank screens for new accounts |
| Conversation scrolls only its own message list after sending (was `scrollIntoView`, which also scrolls the phone frame, the /test frame and the page) | "Blank screen after sending a message". **Not reproduced here** on any message path (profile, house, collaboration, interest reply, inbox) on desktop or mobile, in the app or in /test. This removes the most likely cause on mobile browsers; confirm in the next round |
| Onboarding availability: switching to "Now" clears a typed "from" date | "Availability selection state carrying incorrectly" |
| Screen-level sheets (Discover filters) no longer sit under the floating tab bar | Their actions were covered |

### P0: Onboarding (activation)

V29 had about 11 surfaces before Home, and five of them were profile steps: you, craft, where, looking
for, work.

- Craft was asked twice (main craft and "also do" from the same list).
- Skip meant different things on different steps.
- Media limits were unstated.

V30 keeps the real splash, introduction, account entry, consent and verification. It reduces profile
setup to **two steps** that carry only what personalises the first Home:

1. **About you:** name (required), photo (optional), main craft (required; tap again to clear).
2. **What brings you here?**
   - Intent (required).
   - City (required).
   - "When can you start?", shown only for *Find work* and *Collaborate*.

Then Home. Moved to profile completion, later:

- other crafts and specialisation (now in Edit profile);
- languages and travel;
- bio;
- work and media. "Add your work" stays as one dismissible next step on the first Home, and Edit
  profile → Showreel and work now opens the picker instead of a toast.

There is no Skip in the new steps: required versus optional is stated on the fields. Back keeps every
choice. Media pickers state the limit up front ("Choose up to 10 photos or videos").

### P0: Home

V29 Home had seven sections competing for attention:

- Roles for you
- People worth knowing
- From your network
- Explore your craft
- Projects to watch
- Production houses
- Latest posts

Users read it as a compressed sitemap and as job-heavy even when they were hiring. V30 Home has four
sections, ordered by intent:

1. **Opportunities for you.** Roles, casting and collaborations; a general title because not every
   craft "applies for roles".
2. **People you could work with**, or **People worth knowing** when not assembling a team. Each card
   says why the person is there.
3. **Activity that matters:**
   - credits waiting for your confirmation;
   - people who showed interest in what you posted;
   - professional updates from your network (credits, projects, availability, collaboration
     requests).

   Portfolio and reel uploads are excluded. It is not a feed.
4. **Active projects.** Films and series being made now, hiring first. This replaces "Projects to
   watch", which a participant read as something to stream.

Removed from Home and still in Discover:

- Posts. They no longer compete with professional utility.
- The craft object tiles.
- The separate production houses block. Houses now appear through their projects.

Saved has a visible entry in the Home header (V29: Saved was opened in 1 of 43 sessions).

### P0: Discover

**Discover is the professional database: deliberate search and exploration.** Modes, in order:
**People · Opportunities · Projects · Posts**. The search placeholder changes with the mode.

- **People.** The People mode sits first because finding a person was the most common Discover job.
  - Chip filters: Filters, Available, Production houses, and a craft row with all 15 crafts.
  - Filter sheet: Available, In my city, Verified credits, Open to travel, Experience (under 3, 3 to
    10, 10+ years), Works in (language).
  - Results show availability and verified-credit count.
  - Production houses live here, as a toggle, rather than as a fifth tab.
- **Opportunities.** Renamed from Roles. Adds "My crafts", alongside the existing kind and filter
  sheet.
- **Projects.** All · Being made now · Hiring · Released, and each poster says whether it is hiring.
- **Posts.** Unchanged: a deliberate browsing mode, kept subordinate.

### P1

- **One Saved model** (docs/SAVE_MODEL.md):
  - Covers People, Opportunities, Projects and Posts, organised by type.
  - Saving a person is a private shortlist, never shown to them. Saved from the profile bookmark, and
    from Home's people cards when hiring or collaborating.
  - No folders yet.
- **Profile as professional credibility.** Order:
  1. Identity, craft, location and availability, actions.
  2. Credits waiting for your confirmation (own profile).
  3. **Verified credits**: "Issued by the production and confirmed by X. No one can add their own
     credits."
  4. Showreel and **Selected work**.
  5. About.
  6. Independent projects.
  7. Projects you're on (private, including flagged ones).
  8. Collaborations.
  9. Posts.

  Answers "what have they actually worked on, and can I trust it?" before anything else.
- **Show interest** states the model in three steps:
  1. They see your profile, credits and work.
  2. If it's a fit, they message you; you can't message them from here.
  3. You can withdraw until then.

  Questions say "(optional)", and role questions no longer read as required uploads ("Link a scene…" →
  "A link to a scene…", with "Nothing needs uploading: paste a link if you have one").
- **Bottom navigation:** labelled tabs, a tinted pill behind the active icon, and a bold accent label,
  so Home and Discover always say where you are.

## Rules preserved

- **Credits:**
  - Individuals cannot create or claim credits. Only productions (and their authorised members)
    issue them.
  - People confirm or flag credits.
  - Unchanged; the profile states it more clearly.
- **Interest:**
  1. Show interest makes your profile visible to the poster.
  2. The poster reviews and decides whom to message.
  3. The interested person cannot message the poster from the role.

  A poster may allow direct messages on a collaboration (`allowDM`). Unchanged.
- **Messaging model** (documented, not expanded):
  - Anyone can send a direct message from a profile or a production house page; blocks stop it both
    ways.
  - About a role or collaboration, Show interest replaces messaging, unless the poster allowed direct
    messages.
  - An empty direct thread now says this once.

  No permissions system was added in V30.

## Deliberately not built in V30

Each was considered against the strategy and the research:

- Invite contacts.
- Followers / Following screens.
- A separate watchlist.
- More "View all" destinations.
- New Home modules.
- More feeds.
- Profession-specific Homes.
- Moving notifications.
- Saved folders.
- Availability date ranges.
- Media preview enhancements.

They are P2 at most, and none solves the activation and orientation problems V29 showed.
