# V30: structural iteration

Evidence: *Art & Artist V29 Usability Research Synthesis* (1 Oct 2026; 43 sessions, 1,366 events,
67 feedback records, 85 task attempts). Later-task samples were n = 3, so they are treated as warnings,
not conclusions.

V30 is a structural iteration, not "V29 plus every participant suggestion". The proposition tested
well (7 of 8 first-run respondents rated understanding 4 or 5 out of 5). The problems were orientation,
prioritisation and activation. Every change below names the research problem it answers; anything
without a good answer was not built.

## The model

**Home has two jobs, weighted by how much the product knows about the person:**

- **Established person:** "what matters to me right now?".
- **First-time person:** "is this network worth using?". Their first Home must create the reaction
  "there are useful people, opportunities, projects and creative work here for someone like me".

**Discover answers "let me look for something".**

One adaptable system (`src/domain/relevance.ts`) replaces the idea of a Home per craft. It works from
three inputs:

- **Who you are:** primary craft, other crafts, city, availability, network.
- **What you are trying to do right now (intent):**
  - Find work
  - Find people for a project
  - Collaborate
  - Grow my network
  - Just exploring

  Intent is a state, not a persona.
- **How much is known yet (stage):**
  - *First session:* onboarding answers only.
  - *Early usage:* follows, saves and shown interest start to sharpen ranking. People in crafts you
    follow rank up, and opportunities in crafts you saved rank up.
  - *Established:* an active network.

The modules are the same at every stage and for everyone:

- Opportunities
- People
- Projects, with production houses
- Activity
- Creative work

Intent and stage change their **order**, **titles** and **ranking**. Every Home shows people,
opportunities, projects and creative work somewhere, so nobody mistakes Art and Artist for only a job
board, a hiring directory or a feed. Intent decides priority. Every block says why it is there.

| Intent (first session) | Home order |
|---|---|
| Find work (cinematographer) | Opportunities for you → People in your world → Projects you could join (+ houses) → Happening on Art and Artist → Recent work in cinematography |
| Find work (actor) | Casting and roles for you → Directors and producers → Projects you could join → Happening on Art and Artist → Recent work in acting |
| Find people for a project (director) | People for your next project (Available now filter) → Projects and production houses → Happening on Art and Artist → Opportunities in your craft (close matches only) → Recent work |
| Collaborate | Collaborations and roles → People to collaborate with → Projects → Recent work → Activity |
| Grow my network | People worth knowing → Recent work → Activity → Projects → Opportunities |
| Just exploring | Projects → Recent work → People → Activity → Opportunities |

Established Homes put personal activity higher ("Activity that matters": credits to confirm, interest
in what you posted, network updates). Creative work stays last.

**Nothing goes sparse.** Every list widens step by step and says so:

1. **Exact:** craft, intent, city, availability.
2. **Adjacent:** complementary crafts (camera ↔ lighting ↔ cinematography, edit ↔ colour ↔ VFX,
   sound ↔ music, design ↔ costume ↔ make-up, direction ↔ writing ↔ production), and nearby.
3. **Broader industry:**
   - active projects, then recent releases by verified productions;
   - credible people with verified credits;
   - production houses.
4. **Curated discovery:** strong work from the community.

Specific fallbacks:

- Opportunities never drop below five while anything is open, and widened cards are labelled ("Close
  to your craft", "Open near you").
- A first-time person has no network, so Activity shows public industry activity: "Happening on Art
  and Artist" (credits issued, projects starting, people becoming available).
- A tray that would hold fewer than three items folds into its neighbour.

**Content first, control second.** Home opens with the app header (logo, notifications) and then
straight into the first recommendation section: its title, one compact row, and its cards.

- The compact row holds the intent filter: a small pill such as "Find work ▾" on the first section
  only, which opens the intent sheet. It also holds any section filter and one quiet line on why
  ("Based on your craft, location and availability.").
- There is no personalisation card, no welcome block and no "Cinematographer in Hyderabad" banner.
  Craft and city are used silently.
- The first section starts right under the header, so card imagery fills the first viewport.
- First-session opportunity cards are slightly shorter, so the next kind of value (people) begins
  above the fold.
- The one "Add your work" next step comes after the third module, never before content.
- Saved is not in the Home header. It lives on your own Profile, and objects keep their own Save on
  their detail and profile screens.

**"Available now" is a filter, not a section.** It asked the same question as "People for your next
project" (who could I work with?), only narrowed by availability. It is now an "Available now" pill
in that section's row when assembling a team or collaborating; available people already rank first
and carry an availability badge.

Home stays visually expressive:

- full-bleed opportunity cards;
- portrait trays;
- poster trays for projects;
- media tiles for creative work;
- activity rows with faces and stills.

Curated, professional and creative: not an enterprise dashboard, and not a feed.

**Creative work** is a contained tray: "Recent work in {your craft}", widening to "Work from the
community", at most eight pieces, smaller tiles than projects. It proves the community is active
without becoming the Home feed.

**The 30-second check** (first Home, every intent tested): Are there opportunities for me? Useful
people? Real projects? Does the community feel active? A reason to come back (save, follow, add work)?
Each has a module above or just below the fold.

## Changes, and the problem each one solves

### P0: Functional failures

| Fix | Research problem |
|---|---|
| Every craft on the onboarding list now has at least one opportunity: 8 new seed roles (editor, colourist, VFX, production, director, production design / costume / make-up, sound / music, writing) on existing houses and people. Verified for all 15 crafts | "Suggested roles missing after onboarding": V29 had roles only for cinematography, acting, lighting and camera |
| Respond sheet and Collaboration composer no longer crash for a new account with no work (they assumed a portfolio existed) | Blank screens for new accounts |
| Conversation scrolls only its own message list after sending (was `scrollIntoView`, which also scrolls the phone frame, the /test frame and the page) | "Blank screen after sending a message". **Not reproduced here** on any message path (profile, house, collaboration, interest reply, inbox) on desktop or mobile, in the app or in /test. This removes the most likely cause on mobile browsers; confirm in the next round |
| Onboarding availability: switching to "Now" clears a typed "from" date | "Availability selection state carrying incorrectly" |
| Screen-level sheets (Discover filters) no longer sit under the floating tab bar | Their actions were covered |

### P0: Onboarding (activation)

V29 had about 11 surfaces before Home, and five of them were profile steps: you, craft, where, looking
for, work.

- Craft was asked twice (main craft and "also do" from the same list).
- Skip meant different things on different steps.
- Media limits were unstated.

V30 keeps the real splash, introduction, account entry, consent and verification. It reduces profile
setup to **two steps** that carry only what personalises the first Home:

1. **About you:** name (required), photo (optional), main craft (required; tap again to clear).
2. **What brings you here?**
   - Intent (required).
   - City (required).
   - "When can you start?", shown only for *Find work* and *Collaborate*.

Then Home. Moved to profile completion, later:

- other crafts and specialisation (now in Edit profile);
- languages and travel;
- bio;
- work and media. "Add your work" stays as one dismissible next step on the first Home, and Edit
  profile → Showreel and work now opens the picker instead of a toast.

There is no Skip in the new steps: required versus optional is stated on the fields. Back keeps every
choice. Media pickers state the limit up front ("Choose up to 10 photos or videos").

### P0: Home (see "The model" above for the cold-start rules)

V29 Home had seven sections competing for attention:

- Roles for you
- People worth knowing
- From your network
- Explore your craft
- Projects to watch
- Production houses
- Latest posts

Users read it as a compressed sitemap and as job-heavy even when they were hiring. The problem was
hierarchy, not richness. V30 keeps the same visual language but composes Home from a fixed set of
modules, ordered by intent and stage (see "The model"):

- **Opportunities:** roles, casting and collaborations. The title stays general because not every
  craft "applies for roles"; actors see "Casting and roles".
- **People:**
  - "People in your world", "People for your next project" or "People worth knowing", depending on
    intent, with Follow as the card action;
  - an "Available now" filter when assembling a team or collaborating.
- **Projects:** being made now, hiring first, then the production houses making them, all in one tray of identical cards (same size, 4:5 image, label, title, house, hiring line). No featured first item. This replaces "Projects to
  watch", which a participant read as something to stream.
- **Activity:**
  - "Activity that matters": things you owe, then network updates;
  - "Happening on Art and Artist": public industry activity when there is no network yet.

  Portfolio and reel uploads are excluded. It is not a feed.
- **Creative work:** one contained tray of recent work, subordinate to professional utility.

Moved to Discover: the craft object tiles, and the full posts browsing (Home keeps only the contained
work tray).

Saved lives on your own Profile (People, Opportunities, Projects, Posts). It is not a Home job.

### P0: Discover

**Discover is the professional database: deliberate search and exploration.** Modes, in order:
**People · Opportunities · Projects · Posts**. The search placeholder changes with the mode.

- **People.** The People mode sits first because finding a person was the most common Discover job.
  - Chip filters: Filters, Available, Production houses, and a craft row with all 15 crafts.
  - Filter sheet: Available, In my city, Verified credits, Open to travel, Experience (under 3, 3 to
    10, 10+ years), Works in (language).
  - Results show availability and verified-credit count.
  - Production houses live here, as a toggle, rather than as a fifth tab.
- **Opportunities.** Renamed from Roles. Adds "My crafts", alongside the existing kind and filter
  sheet.
- **Projects.** All · Being made now · Hiring · Released, and each poster says whether it is hiring.
- **Posts.** Unchanged: a deliberate browsing mode, kept subordinate.

### P1

- **People cards on Home:** the card CTA is **Follow** (primary green); tapping the card opens the profile. Saving a person happens on their profile, not on recommendation cards.
- **Profile actions on another person:** **Follow is the primary action** (green). It becomes a
  clear "Following" state. **Message is secondary** (neutral). **Save** is a tertiary utility (the bookmark icon in the profile header). The product encourages building a
  relevant network gradually rather than treating every profile visit as the start of a direct
  message. Whether someone can be messaged is a separate rule (below).
- **One Saved model** (docs/SAVE_MODEL.md):
  - Covers People, Opportunities, Projects and Posts, organised by type.
  - Saving a person is a private shortlist, never shown to them. Saved from the profile bookmark, and
    from Home's people cards when hiring or collaborating.
  - No folders yet.
- **Profile as professional credibility.** Order:
  1. Identity, craft, location and availability, actions.
  2. Credits waiting for your confirmation (own profile).
  3. **Verified credits**: "Issued by the production and confirmed by X. No one can add their own
     credits."
  4. Showreel and **Selected work**.
  5. About.
  6. Independent projects.
  7. Projects you're on (private, including flagged ones).
  8. Collaborations.
  9. Posts.

  Answers "what have they actually worked on, and can I trust it?" before anything else.
- **Show interest** states the model in three steps:
  1. They see your profile, credits and work.
  2. If it's a fit, they message you; you can't message them from here.
  3. You can withdraw until then.

  Questions say "(optional)", and role questions no longer read as required uploads ("Link a scene…" →
  "A link to a scene…", with "Nothing needs uploading: paste a link if you have one").
- **Bottom navigation:** labelled tabs, a tinted pill behind the active icon, and a bold accent label,
  so Home and Discover always say where you are.

## Before you join (consent)

Same consent, lighter presentation. The legal substance is unchanged.

- One title and one line: "A few things to know about how Art and Artist works."
- **What to know:** three compact rows, each with an icon, a title and one line condensed from the V29
  card copy. Tapping a row opens a sheet with the original card text word for word, then the full
  document ("In short" plus the full text).
  - Your work and credits → Terms of Service.
  - Your privacy → Privacy Policy.
  - How people treat each other → Community guidelines.
- **Required consent:** two explicit boxes, never pre-ticked:
  - "I agree to the Terms of Service and have read the Privacy Policy", where both names are links
    that open the documents without ticking the box;
  - "I'm 18 or older".
- **Optional:** "Occasional emails about new features" sits below a divider, labelled Optional, off
  by default.
- "Agree and continue" is enabled only when both required boxes are ticked. Recorded consents are
  unchanged.
- **"Draft for legal review" is internal.** It is no longer shown on any product surface (consent,
  legal documents, house invite, house setup, policy update, Your data). The status is listed in the
  prototype console and remains marked in code (`DraftTag`).
- **Shared by every entry path.** Individuals, invited house members and people setting up a house
  all see the same Before you join. Organisation-specific agreements stay where they apply:
  - "I'm authorised by {house} to act for it" when accepting an invite;
  - "I'm authorised to set up {house}…" when setting up a house.

## Production House parity (V30)

**One product, two professional modes.** House mode shares Discover, profiles, navigation, Saved, the
interest and messaging model, and credits with Individual mode.

Brought to V30:

- **(B)** A house page seen by an individual: **Follow (primary) is the only action.** Individuals do not
  message houses directly; they reach a house through its roles (Show interest), and the house
  decides whom to message. Another house (someone acting for one) can still message it.
- **(B)** People cards on the house Home have Follow (primary) as the card action.
- **(B)** Cold start: a new house is never an empty Home.
  1. "People you could work with" leads (credible professionals across crafts, available and nearby
     first; never the house's own members).
  2. Then "Start a project".
  3. Then "Happening on Art and Artist" when the house has no activity of its own.
- **(C)** Role creation guides questions: answers are optional, and if you want to see work, ask for a
  link, because nothing can be uploaded when showing interest. This stops new "Link a shot"
  ambiguities at the source.

Intentionally different (A):

- **The house Home is a slate.** The lead project is the one being made now, with interest, open
  roles and corrections on it. It is a working object, not an arbitrary featured card. The slate rail
  and open-roles cards are uniform.
- **No intent filter on the house Home.** A house's job is fixed: run its projects and hire.
- **Header:** the identity switcher, notifications and New project. There is no Saved in the header,
  and Saved belongs to the person.
- **Saving while acting for a house** goes to the person's own Saved, as in docs/SAVE_MODEL.md.

## Dates and money inputs

Every date is picked from a calendar and every amount is digits only (`src/ui/fields.tsx`).

- `DateField`: a native date input under the product's own label ("9 Nov"). Tapping it opens the
  platform calendar. Typing is blocked, past days are disabled, and an end date can't come before
  its start.
- `MoneyField`: digits only (letters, symbols and pasted text are stripped), numeric keypad, Indian
  grouping (₹15,00,000). A unit select (/ day, flat, / week, / month) sits next to it.
- Stored text keeps the existing formats ("9 – 21 Nov", "₹15,000–20,000 / day"), so seeded data,
  cards and ranking are unchanged.
- Where it applies: onboarding "When can you start?", the availability sheet, the collaboration
  composer (start, end, amount), a house's Add role (start, end, from and up to amounts) and Edit
  role. A house adding a person's phone number accepts digits and a leading + only.
- Edit role reads dates written in the product format back into the calendar. Looser seeded text
  ("Nov, 9 days") is kept until the house picks real days.

## Rules preserved

- **Credits:**
  - Individuals cannot create or claim credits. Only productions (and their authorised members)
    issue them.
  - People confirm or flag credits.
  - Unchanged; the profile states it more clearly.
- **Interest:**
  1. Show interest makes your profile visible to the poster.
  2. The poster reviews and decides whom to message.
  3. The interested person cannot message the poster from the role.

  A poster may allow direct messages on a collaboration (`allowDM`). Unchanged.
- **Messaging model** (documented, not expanded):
  - Anyone can send a direct message from a person's profile; blocks stop it both ways.
  - Individuals cannot message a production house from its page (Follow only). A house messages people
    (after interest, or directly), and houses can message each other.
  - About a role or collaboration, Show interest replaces messaging, unless the poster allowed direct
    messages.
  - An empty direct thread now says this once.

  No permissions system was added in V30.

## Deliberately not built in V30

Each was considered against the strategy and the research:

- Invite contacts.
- Followers / Following screens.
- A separate watchlist.
- More "View all" destinations.
- New Home modules.
- More feeds.
- Profession-specific Homes.
- Moving notifications.
- Saved folders.
- Availability date ranges.
- Media preview enhancements.

They are P2 at most, and none solves the activation and orientation problems V29 showed.
