# Visual directions

Four looks run on the same screens, content and hierarchy. Switch them in the prototype panel
(Visual direction). Everything lives in `src/styles/looks.css`; each look overrides tokens plus a few
signature details.

| Look | Idea | Signature details | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Minimal | Apple-sterile. Lets photography carry everything. | White, black type, one red accent. | Reads as generic; no identity of its own. |
| Glass | Night shoot. Atmosphere and glow. | Deep brand-tinted gradient, frosted translucent surfaces, white type. | Dark and moody everywhere; blur cost on low-end phones; lower contrast. |
| Bold | Poster room. Graphic and confident. | Matte paper, yellow and brick red, 2.5px black strokes, hard offset shadows, chunky display face, highlighted section titles. | Loud; must be kept disciplined or it becomes childish. |
| Doodle | A cinematographer's notebook. Human, crafted. | Dotted paper, hand-lettered headings with marker underlines, polaroid photos, hand-drawn outlines. | Can feel informal next to verified production houses and money. |
