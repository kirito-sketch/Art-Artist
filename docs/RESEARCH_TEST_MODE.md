# Participant Test Mode (`/test`)

> **Product name: Art and Artist.** Legacy/internal codename: Ana. The codename survives only in
> technical identifiers (for example storage keys, the `ana_research` local database name, the
> `ANA_PROTOTYPE_VERSION` constant and some file names). It never appears in visible UI.

> **Current version: the short five-scenario session, documented in `docs/TEST_REDESIGN.md`.** It covers the participant flow, copy, questions, continuity, I'm stuck, recovery and old-session handling, and it supersedes the flow described below.
>
> The rest of this page describes the **older seven-task study** (`ana-individual-creative-v29`). Its sessions are kept unchanged, and participants already in it carry on with their own tasks. The layout rule, privacy and isolation sections still apply.

An unmoderated, task-based usability test of the real Art and Artist prototype. Participants use Art and Artist itself; a
quiet test layer gives them one task at a time, notices when they succeed, and collects short feedback.
Everything is stored through the research data system (docs/RESEARCH_DATA_SYSTEM.md).

- **URL:** `https://<deployment>/test`. On a static preview, `?test` works too. An optional
  researcher-assigned code: `/test?p=P014`. The Production House study (when active): `/test?type=house`.
- **Code:** `src/research-data/TestMode.tsx` (flow), `src/styles/test-mode.css`, `src/research-data/surface.ts` (routing flags).
- **Not exposed:** the prototype console, persona switcher, Advanced, Edge Case Lab, QA tools,
  researcher controls and debug information. `?lab`, `?scenario`, `?specimen` and `?motion` are ignored
  on `/test`, exactly as in Research Mode.

## Layout rule: research UI never sits inside the phone

The simulated Art and Artist phone renders exactly as it does outside `/test`: the same size (417 × 876 framed on
large screens), the same status bar, header, content and navigation. Nothing belonging to the study is
drawn inside the device, so participants can always tell **Art and Artist** from **the research controls**.

- **Large screens (800 px and wider):** the phone, with a compact floating research panel beside it
  (300 px; warm paper, hairline border, soft shadow, 20 px radius, Art and Artist green for the primary research
  action). The panel holds everything: the task card, Task details, Feedback, I'm stuck, the task
  questions, skip and end.
- **Small screens (under 800 px):** during an active task, a compact bar attached to the browser
  viewport below the device: `Task 2/6 · Find someone…`, plus Feedback and I'm stuck icon buttons, and
  **Done** only on tasks without an automatic success event. Tapping the task, or any research step,
  opens a sheet attached to the **browser viewport**, never to the simulated phone. On phone-width
  screens the device takes the viewport height minus the bar, constant for the whole session, so Art and Artist
  never shifts.
- Between tasks and while answering task questions, the phone stays visible but is not operable
  (`inert`, shown at reduced opacity), so nothing leaks into the next task.
- Product introduction, consent, details, survey and thank-you are research pages shown on their own,
  with no phone.
- Verified on Home, Discover, Role Detail, Profile, Post Detail and the Create sheet, at desktop and
  phone sizes. There were zero research elements inside the phone, the status bar was present, and on
  desktop nothing overlapped the phone screen; the phone matched the normal prototype size exactly. On
  phone size the active-task bar sits fully below the device; only the research sheet (task questions,
  feedback, stuck), which is attached to the browser viewport, covers part of the inert phone.

## Participant flow

1. **Product introduction** ("What is this product?"), on its own page, before consent:
   > **ART AND ARTIST**
   >
   > Art and Artist is a professional network for people working in film and the creative industries.
   >
   > It helps creatives build a credible body of work, discover people and projects, find professional
   > opportunities, and connect with people they may want to work with.
   >
   > During this test, use the prototype as if you were discovering the product for the first time.
   > There are no wrong taps. Some parts of the prototype may still be incomplete.
   >
   > **Continue**

   It is product context only. It never names screens, sections, buttons or navigation, never says
   where a feature lives, and is not repeated before each task. Task instructions likewise never
   give location hints ("go to Discover", "open People", "tap Roles", "use Search").
2. **Consent** ("What information is being recorded?"), see below. A separate page from the
   introduction. Continue is disabled until the box is ticked.
3. **A little about you.** All optional: first name, craft (a short list), experience band. No email,
   phone or demographics. The participant code (P001...) is generated by the server and never shown.
4. **Session starts** (`POST /api/research/session`): the participant row (with `consented_at`), the
   session (study, prototype version, persona `kiran`, device), and the study's tasks.
5. **Task 1, Getting started (the first-run experience).** Framed on its own page, before the phone
   appears: "Task 1 of 7 · Getting started · Imagine you've just discovered Art and Artist and are
   opening it for the first time. Use the app as you normally would. Go through the experience as you
   naturally would until you feel you've reached the main product." **Open Art and Artist** then shows
   the real product at its Splash, followed by the real introduction screens, account entry and
   onboarding (src/entry/Entry.tsx, unchanged). See "First run" below.
6. **Task intro** for every later task (in the panel): "Task 2 of 7", the title and the instruction,
   then **Start task** (primary) or **End test**. Only the current task is shown; later tasks are never listed.
7. **Active task.** Art and Artist is fully usable. The panel shows the task and instruction with **Task details**
   (instruction plus Skip this task), **Feedback** and **I'm stuck**, and End test as a text action.
   **I've finished this task** (or **Done** in the small-screen bar) appears only on tasks without an
   automatic success event (task 2, "Understand Home").
8. **Task questions** (the panel turns into "Task completed" after success, see "Success detection").
9. Repeat for every task, then the **final survey**, then the **thank-you** screen.

The study is chosen by participant type: the newest *active* study of that type
(`GET /api/research/study`). One session holds one study's tasks; the two task sets are never mixed.

## First run (Task 1)

The Individual Creative study has seven tasks. Task 1 is the first-run experience itself; the six
product tasks follow as Tasks 2 to 7 (Understand Home, Find someone in your craft, Find posts, Save a
project or role, Find a role and show interest, Understand your profile).

- **Fresh first-time state.** "Open Art and Artist" starts the product's own entry flow in its
  new-person mode (`startEntry('new')`): splash, then the introduction, entry (Apple, Google, email or
  sign in), consent, verification and onboarding. The prototype state is created fresh for every page
  load and nothing is persisted, so there is no signed-in session, completed onboarding, navigation
  or persona carried over. Nothing outside /test is affected. Research Mode, demo and normal prototype
  use never start the entry flow from here.
- **No research UI in the phone**, no fake onboarding, no hints about Next, Skip, accounts or Home.
- **Success:** the semantic event `first_home_reached`, emitted when the entry flow closes and the main
  product shows (`metadata.via`: `created` for a new account, `signin` for an existing one). The panel
  turns into the first-run questions after the same 1.1 s settle as every task.
- **Measured** (all from existing tables): time from splash to the main product (the attempt's
  `duration_ms`: the attempt starts on Open Art and Artist); screens visited
  (`onboarding_step_viewed` {step: splash, intro_1..3, entry, email, signin, consent, verify, you,
  now}; V29 builds also had craft, where, looking, work: V30 reduced profile setup to two steps,
  see docs/V30.md); backtracking (`onboarding_step_back` {from}); skips
  (`onboarding_step_skipped` {step: intro_n; V29 also looking, work}); abandonment (ending the test during
  Task 1 leaves the attempt `abandoned`); I'm stuck and Feedback during onboarding (normal feedback
  rows attached to the Task 1 attempt).
- **Questions after Task 1** (instead of the standard task questions), stored as feedback rows with
  `source = first_run` attached to the Task 1 attempt:
  1. How easy was it to understand what Art and Artist is for? 1 very difficult to 5 very easy (`understand_product`)
  2. How easy was it to get started? 1 to 5 (`get_started`)
  3. Was anything unclear during the introduction or setup? optional text (`setup_unclear`)
  4. Before continuing, what do you think Art and Artist helps you do? free text, required (`what_it_helps_you_do`)

  "Get started" is also stored as the attempt's `difficulty_rating`, inverted (5 very easy becomes 1)
  so the outcome rule and difficulty averages keep one meaning. "Unclear" is also the attempt's
  `feedback_text`.
- **Skipping Task 1** (I'm stuck or Task details, then Skip) closes the entry flow, so Task 2 starts
  in the product.
- **Participants who started before this change** keep the six-task list their tab received at
  session start ("Task 3 of 6" stays "Task 3 of 6"); they are never sent into onboarding. Their
  sessions and attempts are not modified.
- **Where the task list lives:** `research_tasks` (supabase/seed.sql). The API also keeps it in sync on
  its own (server/research/studies.ts): it adds the Getting started task and moves the six product
  tasks to 2 to 7 by key, idempotently, and never edits or deletes anything else, so no manual SQL is
  needed on deploy.

## Consent copy

> **Before you start**
> - We record what you do in the prototype (screens you open, actions you take) to improve Art and Artist.
> - Anything you choose to write in feedback is stored with it.
> - We never capture passwords or private messages, and nothing you type into the prototype itself.
> - You can stop at any time. What you've done so far is kept.
>
> ☐ I understand and agree to take part.

The session API refuses to start without `consent: true`. The server stores `consented_at`.

## Task model

- Tasks come from `research_tasks` for the study, in `sequence` order, with `success_events`.
- Research UI uses Art and Artist's tokens (paper, hairline, Art and Artist green for the one primary action, existing type),
  but always outside the device (see "Layout rule").

## Success detection

- While a task is active, Test Mode subscribes to the research client's semantic events. When an
  event matches the task's `success_events` (and `success_object_type`, if set), the task has succeeded.
- **Never mid-action:** the panel only turns into the task questions after Art and Artist's own sheet has closed
  (for example the Show Interest sheet after Send), then about 1.1 s more for the transition to settle.
  The phone is never replaced.
- The server marks `success_detected` independently from the same events.
- Seeded tasks: getting started (`first_home_reached`), understand Home (no event: Done), find someone in your craft (`person_profile_opened`),
  find posts (`post_opened`), save a project or role (`project_saved`, `role_saved`), show interest
  (`interest_sent`), your profile (`own_profile_viewed`).

## Task feedback

After each task: "How easy or difficult was that?" on a scale of **1 very easy to 5 very difficult**;
"Was this what you expected to happen?" Yes or No; if No, "What were you expecting?" (optional);
"Was anything confusing?" (optional).

Stored on the attempt:
- `difficulty_rating` (1–5);
- `expectation_match` (Yes = 5, No = 1, using the existing 1–5 column);
- `feedback_text` (`Expected: …` and `Confusing: …` lines).

## Outcome rule

| Situation | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Success (detected, or Done on a task without a success event) and difficulty 1–3 and I'm stuck not used | `completed` |
| Success, and difficulty rated 4–5 **or** I'm stuck used during the attempt | `completed_with_difficulty` |
| Skip task (directly or from I'm stuck) | `skipped`, with the duration and the optional reason in `feedback_text` |
| End test while a task is open | `abandoned` (closed by the server when the session is completed as abandoned) |

Skipped is never counted as success.

## Feedback button

**Feedback** (in the panel, or the viewport sheet on small screens; never a modal inside Art and Artist) → a
category:
- Something was confusing
- I couldn't find something
- Something looked wrong
- Something didn't work
- I have a suggestion
- Other

Then an optional comment, then Send; the panel returns to the active task with "Thanks, your feedback
was sent." Context is attached automatically and the participant is told
so ("We'll know which screen and task you were on"): session, task attempt, current screen, persona,
build (copied from the session by the server), timestamp, and the event position
(`trail_until_sequence`). The trail itself is derived from `research_events` later and is not copied
into the feedback row. Stored with `interaction_context.source = 'button'`.

## I'm stuck and skip

- **I'm stuck** (in the panel or the bar) records `stuck_reported` and asks "What are you trying to do?"
  (optional). Then: **Continue trying** or **Skip task**. A comment is stored as feedback (category
  `couldnt_find`, `source = 'stuck'`). The correct path is never revealed: we are testing findability.
- **Skip task** asks for an optional reason, records `outcome = skipped`, and moves to the next task.

## Ending early

**End test** (from the task intro or the active task panel) asks for confirmation: "Your answers so far will be
kept." The session is completed as `abandoned`. Completed attempts keep their outcomes and the open one
becomes `abandoned`. The participant sees a thank-you screen; nothing is discarded.

## Final survey

1. Overall, how easy was Art and Artist to understand? (1–5, **1 very difficult, 5 very easy**)
2. What do you think Art and Artist is for? (free text)
3. What was the most confusing part? (optional)
4. What, if anything, would make you want to use Art and Artist? (optional)

No schema change was needed. Each answer is a `research_feedback` row with category `other`,
`interaction_context.source = 'survey'`, `question` (`overall_ease`, `what_is_ana_for`,
`most_confusing`, `would_make_you_use`) and, for question 1, `rating`. The dashboard shows them under
"Final survey" and keeps them out of the feedback inbox. A dedicated survey table is a possible later
refinement.

## Home comparison (new Home vs today's Home)

The comparison study (`aa-individual-short-v1`) gives each new session one Home at random, 50/50 (`assignHomeArm` in `src/ui/features.ts`):

- `current`: today's Home;
- `next`: the new Home (fixed frame, composed middle; see `docs/HOME.md`).

The arm:

- is chosen when the participant presses Start;
- is kept in the saved test state, so a reload or resume keeps the same Home;
- is recorded on `session_started` as `metadata.homeVariant`.

Sessions of any other study always see today's Home. Events are off in both arms, so the comparison changes one thing only.

For a pilot, a researcher can pin the arm with `/test?arm=next` or `/test?arm=current`. Don't use it with real participants.

## Session resume

The research client keeps the session token in `sessionStorage` (per tab). Test Mode keeps its stage
and task index there too. A reload or reopening in the same tab resumes at the same step, for example
"Task 4 of 6", and an open attempt is reused, not duplicated. Verified by reloading mid-test. The
prototype's own state (saved items etc.) starts fresh on reload, as it always does. A new tab, or a
closed browser, starts a new session.

## Privacy

- Only semantic events are recorded (docs/RESEARCH_DATA_SYSTEM.md, "Event taxonomy"). Nothing typed
  into the prototype itself is stored: no message text, search text or form contents.
- The only participant words stored are the ones the test asks for: task answers, feedback comments,
  the stuck note, the skip reason and survey answers.
- No email, phone or demographics. The name is optional and shown to researchers only as secondary
  to the participant code.

## Isolation

- The normal prototype and the existing `?research` mode record nothing. Verified: 0 requests,
  0 sessions, 0 events.
- A token left in a tab only counts on the surface that started or resumed it. Opening the normal
  prototype in the same tab after a test records nothing; the client stays inactive.
- `/test` is the only participant surface that starts sessions. Existing Research Mode (`?research`,
  moderated, Aditi for Konark) is unchanged and separate.

## Naming in hosted data

Study titles and task text live in the database. `supabase/seed.sql` now uses "Art and Artist". A
project seeded earlier keeps its old wording until the seed is re-run (it is idempotent: SQL Editor,
paste `supabase/seed.sql`, Run). Until then, Test Mode and the dashboard replace any legacy product name
in database-sourced text before display (`src/research-data/brand.ts`), so nothing visible changes
either way.
