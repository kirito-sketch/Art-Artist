# Professional preferences without live presence

Ana does not expose online status, last seen, live availability, green presence dots or Available pills in either individual or Production House experiences.

Avatar, portrait and people cards no longer accept/render presence decorations. Home/District, house recommendations, Discover/search, profiles, Saved, project/crew views and message-related surfaces share those primitives. Obsolete presence CSS is removed, including filter dots.

Structured professional preferences remain useful for matching: open to work, a future work date, travel preferences, desired work/collaboration and experience. Display these as plain text only where context needs them: **Open to work**, or **Open to work from {date}**. Preferences and onboarding use this language; they do not imply that someone is currently reachable. Follow and Message remain explicit actions.

Availability-related names in older domain/data models are retained to avoid breaking matching and existing preference records. They describe a person's declared professional preferences, never a presence service. Notification unread indicators and verified badges are unrelated and remain intact.

This policy supersedes older Available-pill descriptions in historical District, house and product handoffs. Event request management follows the same rule and exposes profile/request facts without presence badges.
