# 8. Glossary, notifications, success metrics and launch

## Glossary

The words people see in the app. One meaning each.

| Word | Means | Not to be confused with |
|---|---|---|
| **Person** | An individual with a personal account and one professional profile | "User" (never shown) |
| **House** (production house) | A company on Ana with its own page and house account | A person who works there |
| **House account** | The account Art and Artist creates for a production house; its team member signs in to it | A personal account (separate; nobody switches between them) |
| **Project** | A film, series or other production. Shown as Film or Series | "Work" (internal data word, never shown) |
| **Independent project** | A project with no production house behind it. Its credits show as "not verified yet" | |
| **Role** | Someone a house is looking for on a project | "Need" (house-side wording under research) |
| **Interest** | A person telling a house they want a role: their profile plus an optional note | An application (there are no stages or rejections) |
| **Added to a project** | A house has put you on its project privately. Not a credit | A credit |
| **Credit** | A record issued by the production that you worked on a project in a role. Public only once you accept it | A self-claimed entry (impossible) |
| **Verified** | A house Ana has checked; a credit issued by a verified house and accepted | |
| **Open to work** | Your availability, shown on your profile | |
| **Save** | Keep something for later, privately | Follow |
| **Follow** | See someone's new work and activity | Connect (there is none) |
| **Block** | Hide each other everywhere; records stay | Report |
| **Report** | Tell Ana about a problem; you aren't named to the other side | Block |

## Notifications

Who gets told what. Everything in the first three columns works in the prototype (in-app only) unless marked proposed. The channel column is a proposal for the native app, where push is new.

| Event | Who is notified | Where it opens | Proposed channel (native app) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Someone shows interest in a role | The house | Review interest | In-app + push |
| A house messages you | The person | Conversation | In-app + push |
| A person messages a house | The house | Conversation | In-app + push |
| A role you're interested in is filled or closed | Everyone else who showed interest | Role page | In-app |
| A role you're interested in changed dates or location | Not a notification today: the role page says "Updated since you showed interest" | Role page | In-app (proposed) |
| You were added to a project | The person | Project you're on | In-app + push |
| "This isn't right" on an association | The house | Workspace | In-app |
| A credit was issued to you | The person | Waiting for you | In-app + push |
| A correction was asked | The house | Workspace | In-app + push |
| A corrected credit was issued | The person | Waiting for you | In-app + push |
| A credit was accepted | The house | Workspace | In-app |
| Someone followed you | The person | Their profile | In-app |
| Terms changed | Everyone, as an agreement screen at next sign-in (not a notification) | Agreement screen | In-app |
| House verified or needs more evidence (proposed, not built) | House owner | House settings | In-app + email |

Not notified: a withdrawn interest, "Not now", and anything about a person who has blocked you.

## Success metrics for v1

Proposed; to be agreed on Sunday.

| Goal | Measure |
|---|---|
| People join and complete their profile | Sign-ups · share who finish onboarding · share with a showreel or selected work |
| Houses use Ana to hire | Verified houses · roles published per house per month |
| Roles get answered | Interest per role · share of roles with at least one conversation |
| Hiring happens through Ana | Roles closed as "Filled through Ana" |
| Credits become a trusted record | Credits issued · share accepted within 7 days · corrections per 100 credits |
| People come back | Weekly returning people · time to a new person's first interest or conversation |
| Safety works | Reports handled within 24 hours |

## Launch plan

Proposed; to be agreed on Sunday.

1. **Closed beta** with a handful of verified houses (sales-led) and the people they work with, in the launch cities.
2. **Seed real content** with those houses: their projects, open roles and credits, so new people see real value on their first Home.
3. **Open sign-up** for individuals in the launch cities once houses have live roles.
4. **Widen** to more cities and houses; add events and film reviews (v1.1).

**For approval:** the glossary, the notification list, the success metrics and the launch approach.
