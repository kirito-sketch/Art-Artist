# 6. Epics for v1

What v1 has to do, as epics with the key acceptance points the client approves. Detailed user stories are written after Sunday, for v1 only, and approved in batches.

| # | Epic | Acceptance points |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Join and sign in** | Apple, Google and email with a code · returning people land in their account whichever way they sign in · an unfinished sign-up resumes where it stopped · agreement recorded with version and date · 18+ confirmed · Back never re-enters a code screen |
| E2 | **Professional profile** | Name, crafts, city, availability, about, showreel, selected work · verified credits shown separately from independent projects · privacy choices · edit in sections with save and discard |
| E3 | **Discover and search** | Posts, People, Opportunities, Projects, Events, Reviews in one place · search across everything · craft filters · blocked people and minors never appear |
| E4 | **Personal Home** | City header and tiles, This week, a section that follows why you're here, From your network, Events near you, Film talk, New work, People near you · follows the ranking goals and guardrails agreed on Sunday · new people get a useful first Home |
| E5 | **Opportunities and Interest** | Role page with status · Show interest with optional note and short answers · withdraw with undo until a conversation starts · told the outcome when a role is filled or closed |
| E6 | **Production house accounts** | Created by Art and Artist's team after the sales visit; houses can't sign up · team member(s) sign in to the house account (how many: B9) · verified before public roles, credits and search (B11) · internal tool for the sales team to create and manage house accounts |
| E7 | **House projects and roles** | Private projects with working titles · publish, edit, close and reopen roles under the role rules · review interest without stages or rejections · message as the house |
| E8 | **Team and credits** | Add people privately · issue or prepare credits by permission · accept, correct, amend · nothing public until accepted · accepted credits never deleted |
| E9 | **Posts and availability** | Post photos and video from the device, reorder, reposition, cover · Open to work · tall images never cropped |
| E10 | **Messaging** | Person ⇄ person and person ⇄ house conversations · start a conversation from the Inbox · reply as the house · mute, report, block · failed messages retry · blocking closes both ways |
| E11 | **Notifications** | One list per account · Needs you on top · mark all as read · push never shows a private project's title (proposed) |
| E12 | **Trust and safety** | Report with reason and promise · block everywhere · moderation console with audit log · moderators can't delete credits |
| E13 | **Account and data** | Settings, privacy, blocked people · your data · delete account under the agreed rule · re-consent when terms change |
| E14 | **Project page and trailers** | Public project page with trailer, credits and open roles · trailer required for public projects in post-production or released · persistent media storage |

Proposed for v1.1: **Events**, **Film reviews**.
Not in v1: film news, fine arts, paid features, bulk credits, applicant tracking.

**For approval:** the epic list and their acceptance points, and what is out of v1.
