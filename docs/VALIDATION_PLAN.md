# Production house validation plan (Version 18)

The exact script, observation sheet, participant record and synthesis table are in docs/research/. Set up with **Start research session** in the prototype controls (or add `?research` to the URL): it opens clean as Aditi acting for Konark, with no controls. Reload between participants.

## Setup

- **Who:** 5 to 6 people from 3 to 4 Telugu, Malayalam or Hindi production houses. Mix owners/producers with the people who actually handle crew and credits (line producers, production executives, coordinators).
- **Format:** 60 minutes, in person, on a phone. One facilitator, one note-taker. Recorded with consent.
- **Prototype:** persona "Konark Pictures" in the panel, Minimal, primary design. Reset before each session.
- **Rule:** don't explain Ana first. Say only: "This is an app where film companies and film professionals find each other. Please think aloud." Give tasks, then watch.

## Opening (10 min): today's reality, before the prototype

- Walk me through the last time you needed a crew member you didn't already know. What did you do?
- Who in your company handles that? On whose phone?
- When does someone count as "on the film" for you? Is it a contract, a WhatsApp group, a call sheet?
- How are final credits decided today? Who signs off? When?
- Has anyone ever told you their credit was wrong? What happened?

## Tasks (35 min)

Read each task, hand over the phone, stay quiet. Ask "what did you expect?" only after they finish or get stuck.

| # | Task (read aloud) | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "You've just started a new Telugu film. It isn't announced. Put it on Ana." | What they expect to enter. Whether "working title" and "private" make sense. What's missing. |
| 2 | "You need a Colourist, but you can't announce the film yet. Find someone using Ana." | Do they create a role, or search people? Does "Untitled Telugu feature" feel safe? Do they read the disclosure? Would they keep a role private? |
| 3 | "Someone good has shown interest. Choose them." | Is a profile plus note enough, or do they want a CV or application? Do they understand "Filled through Ana" and adding the person? |
| 4 | "That person has started working with you. Where do they appear?" | Does "On this project / Added" match anything real? Do they expect the person to be told? Would they use it or skip it? |
| 5 | "The film is done and you know their final credit. Give it to them." | When they'd really do this. What they call the credit. Who does it. One at a time or all at once. |
| 6 | "You're ready to announce. Put it out on Ana." | What they expect to become public. Do they trust the preview? Is anything surprising? |
| 7 | "Someone says their credit is wrong." (verbal) | How they expect it to be fixed. Who decides. What should happen to the public record meanwhile. |

## Close (10 min)

- Show the house Home (Slate): "What is this screen telling you?" (not "do you like it")
- Show the house page: "If a colourist opened this, what would they learn about you?"
- "What would stop your company from using this?"
- "Who else in your company would need to use it?"

## Questions to answer across interviews

Operations: who operates Ana for the house · whose device · how many people need access · who creates projects · at what stage a project goes in.
Roles: would they recruit before announcing · keep roles private · is Interest enough or do they need structured questions.
Records: when someone is "on the project" · when credits are issued · who approves wording · who should hold authority · how co-productions handle it · how often the person isn't on Ana · would bulk issuance help.
Experience: projects first or activity first when opening the app · does Slate feel natural · does Work first fit their public page.

Avoid "Do you like this?" Prefer "Show me how you'd do X", "What would you expect here?", "How do you do this today?", "Who normally handles this?"

## What the first two interviews must tell us

These decide whether the model holds before anything else is built.

1. **Who holds credit authority.** Owner only, a producer, or a line producer? Is it per project? (If it's always one senior person, "prepared credits" is central; if anyone can, it's noise.)
2. **When "on the project" happens, and whether houses would record it at all.** If they'd never add people before credits, the association record may be unnecessary overhead.
3. **Would they post a role for an unannounced film, and is "Untitled Telugu feature" safe enough?** This decides whether private-project recruiting is a core pattern.
4. **Is Interest enough to choose someone?** If both ask for a CV or answers every time, structured responses move up.
5. **When and how final credits are decided.** Individually during the shoot, or as a list at wrap? This decides whether bulk issuance is needed.
6. **Who actually holds the phone.** This decides how prominent identity switching must be.

## Remaining assumptions, ranked by risk

### High: if wrong, the model changes
- **Production-side credit authority.** We assume one house issues credits and some members hold authority. Co-productions, or credits decided by a studio or platform, could break this.
- **Houses will record associations before credits.** If they won't, "Added to project" is overhead and the credit flow must stand alone.
- **Independent-project credit authority.** Unsolved. A large share of early-career work is independent; without a trusted issuer, those people have thin verified records.
- **Houses will put unannounced projects into Ana at all.** Confidentiality culture may mean projects only enter at announcement, which weakens private projects and private roles.

### Medium: the model stays, an important workflow changes
- **Interest vs structured responses.** Busy roles may need required, comparable answers.
- **Private-project public roles.** The pattern may be wanted, or seen as a leak risk.
- **Project publication lifecycle.** Cancellation and archive rules are proposed, not tested.
- **Linking people not on Ana.** If most crew aren't on Ana at first, the invite and linking flow becomes central to adoption.
- **Identity-aware notifications.** One filtered surface may confuse people who act for more than one house.
- **Timing of credit issuance** (individual vs bulk at wrap).

### Low: presentation, terminology, refinement
- Slate as the house Home (vs activity-first).
- Work first as the house page hierarchy.
- The words "Added to project", "On this project", "Working title", "Share on Ana", "Filled through Ana".
- The Minimal visual baseline.
