# Production house interview script (60 minutes)

**Prototype:** open Ana with **Start research session** (or `?research`). It starts as Aditi Ghosh acting for Konark Pictures, with Neela Akasham in pre-production (two open roles, interest waiting), Kaal as an unannounced series, and a realistic set of people. **Reload the page before every participant**; nothing carries over.
**Roles:** facilitator (talks, never helps) and note-taker (fills the observation sheet, docs/research/OBSERVATION_SHEET.md).
**Words to avoid until the participant uses them:** "Added to project", "association", "credit authority", "interest model", "private project", "Slate", "Need".

---

## Before the session (2 min, researcher only)

- Reload the prototype. Check the header reads "Konark Pictures · Acting for the house".
- Phone at full brightness, screen recording on (with consent), notifications silenced.

## Introduction (3 min)

> Thanks for making time. We're designing an app for people who make films, and we'd like to learn how your company actually works. There are no right answers, and nothing you say is a test of you. If something is confusing, that's the app's fault, not yours.
>
> For the first part we'll just talk about how you work today. Then I'll give you a phone and a few situations to try. I won't explain the app; please think out loud as you go. Is it okay if we record the screen and audio? Only our small team will see it.

---

## Part 1: Current behaviour (15 min). Don't show or mention Ana.

Ask as a conversation. Follow the interesting answers; you don't need every question.

**Starting a project**
1. Tell me about the last project your company started. How did it begin inside the company?
2. At that stage, who knows about it? What name do people use for it internally?
3. When does a project become public? Who decides?

**Finding crew**
4. Think of the last time you needed someone for a key role and didn't already know who. Walk me through what happened.
5. Who in the company did that? Whose phone were the calls and messages on?
6. How do you find someone outside your usual network?
7. Have you ever looked for someone while the film was still secret? How did you describe the project?

**Choosing and attaching people**
8. When someone gets in touch about a role, what do you need to see to decide whether to talk to them?
9. At what point is someone "on the film" for you? What makes it official: a call, a contract, a WhatsApp group, the call sheet?
10. Where do you keep track of who's working on a film?

**Credits**
11. How are the final credits put together? Who writes them, and when?
12. Who has the final say on a credit's wording?
13. Has anyone told you their credit was wrong? What happened?
14. On a co-production, who decides the credits?

**Working titles**
15. Do the crew know the real title while working? Do you ever use codenames? Is there anyone working on a film who shouldn't know the title?

---

## Part 2: Prototype tasks (30 min)

Hand over the phone on the Konark Home. Read each task exactly. **Don't help.** If they're stuck for about a minute, ask "What are you looking for?" and note it as a hesitation. If they're still stuck, say "Let's move on" (mark the task **failed**). After each task, ask only: "What did you expect to happen?"

| # | Say exactly | Success looks like | Note especially |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "You've just started a Telugu film that hasn't been announced yet. Put it into Ana." | A new project created (+ or "New project") | What they want to type. Do "working title" and "only Konark sees this" make sense? What's missing? |
| 2 | "You need a Colourist for this film, but the film itself is still secret. Find a way to start looking." | A role added and opened on Ana, after reading what becomes visible | Do they add a role or search people? Do they read the disclosure? Does "Untitled Telugu feature" feel safe? Would they keep it private? |
| 3 | "Someone suitable has responded. Decide what you want to do with them." Before this task, the researcher opens Neela Akasham, where people have already responded. Say: "Let's use this film, where people have responded." | Reviews interest; messages someone, or closes the role as filled and picks a person | Is a profile plus note enough? Do they ask for a CV, rate or showreel? Do they understand "Filled through Ana"? |
| 4 | "They're now working on the film. Show me what you'd expect to happen next." | Adds the person to the project, or says what they'd do | Don't say "added to project". Does it map to anything real? Do they expect the person to be told? Would they skip it? |
| 5 | "The film is finishing and their final screen credit is now known. Record it." | Issues a credit with a credit line | What they call it. When they'd really do this. Who in the company would. One at a time or all at once? |
| 6 | "The film can now be announced publicly." | Shares on Ana after reading the preview | What they expect to become public. Do they trust the preview? Any surprises (the working title, people)? |
| 7 | "There's a mistake in someone's credit on Neeli Raat. Fix it." (If needed: "Sameer says his credit is wrong.") | Finds the correction on Neeli Raat and accepts or changes it | How they expect corrections to work. Who decides. What should happen publicly meanwhile. |

---

## Part 3: Reflection (10 min). Only after the tasks.

Point to things; don't ask for ratings.

- **Home:** go back to Home. "What is this screen telling you?" / "What would you look at first each morning?"
- **Project model:** "When you added the Colourist to the film, what did that mean to you?" / "What would happen next in real life?"
- **Interest:** "When someone showed interest, what did you know about them? What would you normally want before calling?"
- **Credits:** "Who at your company would actually issue that credit?" / "When would they do it?"
- **Organisation identity:** "Whose phone would this be on? Who else would need to use it?" / "If two people ran Konark on Ana, what would worry you?"
- **Personal vs company actions:** "If you followed a cinematographer while using Konark, whose follow would that be?"
- **Public company page:** open the Konark page. "If a colourist opened this, what would they learn about you?" / "Is anything here you wouldn't want public?"
- **Expected but missing:** "Was there anything you expected to find and couldn't?"

Never ask "Did you like it?" Prefer "What did you expect here?", "What would you normally call this?", "Who would do this at your company?", "What would happen next in real life?"

## Close (2 min)

> That's everything. Is there anything about how your company works that we should have asked? Can we follow up if we have one more question?

After the participant leaves: complete the participant record (docs/research/PARTICIPANT_RECORD.md) within 24 hours, then update the synthesis table. **Don't redesign after a single interview.**
